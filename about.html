About

A builder first.

I'm Punch, a small builder and brand scaler. I build websites from scratch and help brands grow, and I've been doing it on the web for two years.

My story

Learning by building.

I've spent the last two years working on the web: designing sites, writing code and building brands. I learned the way most builders do, by making real things, shipping them and fixing what didn't work.

Before focusing on websites, I ran my own business, and it generated over $10,000. Running it taught me something a lot of designers miss: a website isn't decoration. It has to earn trust, explain what you do in seconds, load fast and turn visitors into customers.

That's still how I think about every project. I care about how a site looks, but I care more about what it does for the brand behind it.

Today I work across design, code and growth. I'd rather understand the whole picture than hand off half of it, so I handle projects myself from the first sketch to launch.

How I work.

A few rules I stick to on every project.

From scratch, every time

No templates or recycled themes. Every site is designed and coded for the brand it's for, so it doesn't look like everyone else's.

One person, start to finish

You deal with the person actually building your site. Nothing gets lost between a sales team, a designer and a developer.

Honest about time

I'm building multiple websites right now and my schedule is strict. I only take on work when I can give it proper attention, and I'll tell you upfront if I can't.

Built to be used

A site should be easy for your customers to use and easy for you to keep up to date. I build with both in mind.
