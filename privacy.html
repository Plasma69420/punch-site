Privacy policy.

Last updated November 2026.

This website is run by Punch. This page explains what information the site handles and why.

The contact form

The contact form is not live yet, so nothing you type into it is sent or stored. When it goes live, it will collect the details you choose to enter, such as your name, email address, project details, budget range and, for businesses or non-profits, information about your organization. That information will only be used to reply to you and to scope your project. It will not be sold or shared.

Analytics

The site uses an anonymous visit counter. It counts visits and does not collect your name, email address or other personal details.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts, to load the fonts used on the site.
  • An anonymous counter service, to count visits.
  • Discord and Lanyard, to show my Discord profile picture and online status.

Cookies and storage

The site stores a small value in your browser so a visit is only counted once per session. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your information

Once the contact form is live, you can ask for any enquiry you've sent to be deleted by replying to the email you receive.
