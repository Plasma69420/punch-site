privacy policy

last updated october 2026

this site is run by punch (heyimpunch.xyz). this page explains what information it handles.

what i collect

only what you choose to type into the request form: the service you want, a description, optional links, your budget, your timeline and your discord username. that is used to reply to you and to scope the work.

how it is sent

requests are delivered to me privately on discord, or copied by you and sent to me on discord yourself. i do not sell or share your request with anyone else.

third-party services

  • an anonymous page-view counter (counts visits, no personal details).
  • a live discord status service that shows whether i am online.
  • google fonts and discord's image servers for fonts and my profile picture.

cookies and storage

the site stores a few small values in your browser (for example, so a visit is only counted once per session and the request form can limit repeat sends). there are no advertising or tracking cookies.

deleting your information

message me on discord and i will delete any request you sent.
