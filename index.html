About

A builder first.

I've spent the last two years working on the web: designing sites, writing code and building brands. Everything I make is built from scratch, without templates, so each project fits the brand it's for.

Before this, I ran my own business, and it generated over $10,000. Running it taught me what a website actually needs to do for a brand: look right, load fast, earn trust and bring people in. I build with that in mind.

I do a lot of different things. Design, front-end and back-end code, the systems behind a brand, and the growth side once it's live. I'd rather understand the whole picture than hand off half of it.

Right now I'm in the middle of creating multiple websites, including my own brand, Larp Hub. My schedule is busy and I keep it strict, so I take on new work carefully and only when I can give it proper attention.
