Types of websites.

Every site is built for its purpose. Here's what each type usually involves.

01

Business websites

A clear, professional home for your company that explains what you do and builds trust.

  • Home, about, services and contact pages
  • Clear calls to action
  • Contact or enquiry forms
02

Online stores

Storefronts that make it simple for people to find a product and check out.

  • Product and collection pages
  • Cart and checkout flow
  • Payment integration
03

Portfolio & personal

Sites that show off your work or your personal brand properly.

  • Project and case study pages
  • Custom layouts per project
  • Bio and contact
04

Landing pages

A single focused page for a launch, a product or a campaign.

  • One clear goal per page
  • Fast loading
  • Sign-up or purchase focus
05

Web apps & dashboards

Sites that do more than display: accounts, members' areas and tools.

  • Logins and members' areas
  • Dashboards and tools
  • Custom back-end logic
06

Brand scaling

Help growing a brand once the site is live, from messaging to launch.

  • Positioning and messaging
  • Launch planning
  • Ongoing improvements

Common questions.

Are you taking orders right now?

Not yet. I'm building multiple websites at the moment, so orders aren't open. You can still send your details through the contact form, and I'll be in touch when they open.

Do you use templates or page builders?

No. Every site is designed and coded from scratch for the brand it's for.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size and features. A landing page is much quicker than an online store or web app. You'll get a timeline with your quote, before anything starts.

How much does a website cost?

Each project is quoted individually based on scope. Use the budget range in the contact form to give me an idea of what you're working with.

Do you work with businesses and non-profits?

Yes. The contact form lets you say whether the site is personal, for a business or for a non-profit, so I can ask the right questions.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of the project, examples of sites you like, any branding you already have (logo, colours, photos), and a rough budget and timeline.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Add the link to your current site in the contact form and tell me what you'd like to change.
